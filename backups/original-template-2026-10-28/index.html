03 — About

Designer by trade, curious by nature.

Lisbon studio, 2026

I'm Mara — an independent designer and art director working between Lisbon and everywhere else. For the past eight years I've helped founders, studios and cultural institutions turn good ideas into brands and websites that feel unmistakably theirs.

My approach is equal parts strategy and instinct. I ask a lot of questions, sweat the small stuff, and believe the best work happens when design has a job to do — and a little room to play.
